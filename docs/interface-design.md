# MoFlo Cloud interface

The application uses the owner's MoFlo Cloud screenshots as its visual reference: a compact header, a narrow icon navigation rail, blue accents, a light gray canvas, and white cards with rounded borders. The existing company knowledge, evidence and export functions remain the source of truth.

## Figma references

The following frames were read through the Figma connector:

- [Cloud workspace and company cards, frame 24:2697](https://www.figma.com/design/QwMAzEWQTK8nSiFjojBfQS/MoFlo?node-id=24-2697) documents the signed-in workspace, navigation, General Info, brand presentation and observed typography. It specifies Poppins for the application, a 30px / 37.5px company title at weight 600, and uppercase 14px / 20px card headings at weight 600.
- [Public Knowledge page audit, frame 7:300](https://www.figma.com/design/QwMAzEWQTK8nSiFjojBfQS/MoFlo?node-id=7-300) records the public marketing page's section layout and evidence. It describes a different page from the authenticated workspace.

These are design audit boards. Their Inter annotation typography and decorative board backgrounds are not application components. The owner's screenshots provide the application layout reference; the workspace audit supplies the documented typography and component observations. Poppins is served locally, while the existing Noto Sans files remain available to document exports.

## Navigation and page layout

- The shared header identifies MoFlo Cloud and provides the real account action. The rail links to the company list and, when a company is selected, its workspace and knowledge viewer.
- The company dashboard retains company search, creation, archive filtering and workspace access.
- The workspace uses a compact category selector instead of the previous large category sidebar. General Info presents company facts in a summary banner and grouped cards.
- Customers & Industry and Sales & Marketing organize existing fields into focused cards. They share the underlying Market & Customers entry; an entry's save and review controls apply to that whole entry, as explained in the interface.
- Business Offerings uses a selectable list and one offering editor at a time. Switching offerings preserves pending edits. Each offering retains its own review and history.
- Branding, people, source evidence, scrape controls, proposals, version publication and document exports remain available. Site Knowledge opens the existing knowledge viewer.
- Small screens stack cards and offering panels; navigation and actions remain accessible without requiring a desktop viewport.

## Knowledge viewer at `/knowledge/view`

- The viewer presents one company's knowledge, from the current draft or a saved version, in the same frame as the workspace: a top row with a "Company" tab on the left and the "Knowledge section" selector on the right, one large white card, and a tip bar beneath it.
- The "Knowledge section" control is a listbox. Its trigger is a button (`aria-haspopup="listbox"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`) named "Knowledge section" that shows the current section. It opens a `role="listbox"` of eight `role="option"` items, with `aria-selected` on the current one, in the reference order: General Info, Brand Identity, Business Offerings, Customers & Industry, Sales & Marketing, Key People, Site Knowledge and All sections. The `section` URL parameter (`general`, `brand`, `offerings`, `customers`, `sales`, `key-people`, `site`, `all`) selects one; a missing or unknown value opens General Info. ArrowUp and ArrowDown, Home and End move through the options, Enter or Space selects, and Escape or a click outside closes the list (Escape returns focus to the trigger). The trigger and list carry `data-state="open|closed"`, which drives a short transition that respects `prefers-reduced-motion`. The page reads the parameter on the server, and choosing an option updates the URL without reloading, keeping `companyId`, `versionId` and any other parameters with a single `section`. Key People is selectable and shows its heading with "No key people yet" and a "+ Add" link to the editor's `key_person` category; its content is built later. Business Offerings also reads an `offering` parameter (an offering's entry id) the same way; a missing or unknown id selects the first offering.
- Each section opens with a pale-blue summary banner followed by a grid of cards. General Info shows the company name with Founded, Employees and Legal entity, then Headquarters, Alternate names, Overview and Founding story. Customers & Industry shows the industry with counts of groupings, locations, buyers and suppliers, then Business model, Company role, Industry outlook and chip cards for Industry groupings, Service locations, Buyers and Suppliers. Sales & Marketing shows the company name with one column per social link, then Pitch, Ideal persona, Customer needs, Channels, Calls to action and Funnels.
- Business Offerings opens with the heading, a count badge, a short read-only subtitle and a primary "+ Add Offering" link to the offering editor. Below, a list named "Business offerings" holds every offering (its name, or "Untitled offering N" for its position, with its type as a subtitle) and the selected one, the first by default, is tinted pale blue and marked `aria-current`; each row is a button, so the list works from the keyboard, and choosing one updates the `offering` URL parameter without reloading. The detail beside it shows the selected offering's Offering (name), Type, Description, Key features, Cost and Pricing model as read-only boxes; Cost is the price wording, else amount with currency and billing interval, else "Free" or "Contact for pricing". There are no images and no Delete action. Empty fields in the detail show a "+ Add" link that opens the editor on that offering (`entryId` in the link); the top "+ Add Offering" link is not tied to an offering. A company with no offerings shows "No offerings yet" with "+ Add" and a count of 0.
- Brand Identity opens with a banner of four cells: "Brand Identity", the company name and a "Display name" caption; the "Logo" cell (the first logo as an image, or its label when its address is not http or https); the "Palette" cell (the first three colors as adjoining swatches); and the primary font name beside an "Aa" tile. Below, a Logos card (tiles labelled Primary, the logo's variant or "Logo N"), a Colors card (swatch with a hex chip, then the color's role or Primary, Secondary, Tertiary, "Color N", and its hex) and a full-width Fonts card (Primary or "Font N", the family, and the sample sentence "The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog"), each with a dashed add tile linking to the branding editor. Images use only http(s) addresses and a style attribute only ever carries a color that is exactly `#rrggbb`; the sample sentence is therefore shown in the page's own font, not the brand font. Website observations are not presented as confirmed brand guidance: a color classified as observed carries "Observed website color" and is labelled "Color N" (or its apparent role), never Primary, Secondary or Tertiary; a font not marked brand-confirmed carries "Observed, not confirmed as brand guidance" with how it was observed and is labelled "Font N"; the banner palette and font cells say so too, and the qualifier is part of each item's evidence-control name. Card subtitles do not claim the values are used by the brand.
- Values are text, never form controls. A card with no value shows a dashed empty state with a "+ Add" link to the editor, opened on the entry kind that holds the field. Chip cards show one outlined chip per item and a dashed add chip. Missing banner stats show "+ year", "+ count" and similar links. Nothing is invented: there is no revenue figure because the data model has none.
- Evidence stays with the values. Each card, and each banner stat, carries the existing evidence control at its right edge, for the draft and for saved versions: it opens onto the exact source excerpt, source link, user correction and the "Historical generated" or "Historical inferred" label, and its label names every origin present in the field. In list fields (including offerings in the list, key features, logos, colors and fonts), each item whose own claim is a user correction or historical generated/inferred wording also carries a small marker, so the closed view shows which item has which origin. Where a field is empty, partial or contested, the reason is printed with it (in the card, under the banner stat, or in the banner for social links) ("Unknown · Not assessed", "Partial", "Conflicting sources", "Not found in checked pages"), because unknown is not evidence of absence. The latest-crawl source-coverage panel sits under the tip bar. A persona with no name or role is shown under its organization type or a numbered neutral heading; no name is invented.
- Site Knowledge is one card headed "Sitemap / Internal Links" with a muted description. It lists every stored link in stored order: the title in bold, the address as an external link (opened in a new tab with `rel="noopener noreferrer"`) only when it is http or https, otherwise as plain text, and the note when there is one. A "+ Add page" link to the editor on the `site_links` entry sits at the card's right edge, in a draft and in a saved version alike. With no entry or no links the card says "No internal links yet" and prints the recorded coverage reason; a saved version made before this section existed shows the same empty state. Each link whose own claim is a user correction (or historical inferred/generated wording) carries a marker named "<origin>: evidence for <title>", and the card carries the field-level evidence control.
- "Import from website" appears beside "+ Add page" only in an editable draft (not in a saved version, for a viewer, or for an archived company). It opens a modal dialog that lists the latest crawl's captured pages that are plain http(s) addresses without credentials and are not already stored links, each as a checkbox labelled with the page title (or its address) and showing the address. "Add selected" saves the checked pages after the stored links, one new link each (fresh id, the page title or address as title, no note), as a new entry when none exists or as the next revision of the existing one, with the same request shapes and provenance handling as the editor; the dialog then closes and the card shows the new links. Nothing is sent when nothing is checked or on Cancel or Escape, a second click while saving is ignored, a failed save shows the server's message and keeps the dialog, and a 409 asks the person to reload. A list never grows beyond 500 links: the picker stops accepting checks at the limit and says "Only N more pages can be added (maximum 500 links)."
- All sections stacks the seven sections (including Key People) in listbox order, each under a visible heading (Business Offerings keeps its own heading with its count), with the same cards as the section alone and a single "Import from website" control.
- The version selector, company switcher, display modes (Profile, Cards, Table, Detail, Json), document export, JSON download and "Open current draft" sit in a slim toolbar above the card. Profile is the reference layout; the other modes keep their search and filter row. In a draft, a collapsed "Review draft entries" list under the tip bar offers Edit draft, History and Remove from future versions for every entry.

## Scope

This is a presentation change. It does not add artificial intelligence, notification counts, external applications or placeholder content from the reference images. Company facts and counts come from the current draft. Existing membership permissions, conflict handling, citations, revisions, unsaved navigation warnings and immutable saved versions continue to apply.

Verification uses the existing unit suite, type checking, lint and production build, followed by a representative browser walkthrough of category navigation, offering selection, edits, save/review controls and exports. Screenshots record the resulting interface; visual details do not require snapshot tests.
